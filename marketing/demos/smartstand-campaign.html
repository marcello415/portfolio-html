The SmartStand campaign, end to end

One Rainin campaign, from the product page to the lead follow-ups. SmartStand was a new pipette stand, and EasyDirect was the software that went with it. Each step shows the real piece and what my part was.

Where
Mettler-Toledo Rainin, as Web Producer
Product
SmartStand and EasyDirect pipette management
My role
Product page, emails, follow-ups, markets, reporting
Tools
AEM, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Welocalize, Adobe Analytics

The product page is a saved copy from April 2019. The emails are rebuilt from mt.com/rainin assets on one modular template; subject lines, dates and the contact name are examples.

  1. Step 1 · The plan

    One product, several pieces

    A new product needed a page to land on, an email to announce it, content to draw people in, and a follow-up for everyone who raised a hand. Every piece pointed back to the same page.

    My partBuilding and running each piece with a small team: web, email and follow-up.
    1. 01Product pageSmartStand on mt.com/rainin
    2. 02Launch emailNew product announcement
    3. 03Content offersWhite papers behind a short form
    4. 04Follow-upHot and cold lead emails
    5. 05ResultsDashboards, then the next send
  2. Step 2 · The product page

    Everything points here

    The SmartStand page was built in AEM from the component library: a hero, feature blocks, products and specs, documents, service and the EasyDirect videos. Images came from the DAM.

    My partManaging the page and its assets in AEM, and keeping it in step with the online store.
    Open the page →
  3. Step 3 · The launch email

    Announce it

    The announcement used the product page’s own copy and images, so the email and the page said the same thing. One button: Explore SmartStand.

    My partBuilding the email on the modular template in Salesforce Marketing Cloud, testing and sending.
    Open the email →
  4. Step 4 · Content offers

    Give people a reason to fill out a form

    White papers on pipette management and quality were offered free behind a short form. Each email carried an EasyDirect block, and each form submission became a lead.

    My partSending the white paper promos and publishing the pages they linked to.

    Put the product in other sends

    Other emails could carry the product too. The free 2017 “Art of Pipetting” calendar email had a SmartStand block under the calendar.

    My partAdding the SmartStand block to the calendar email.
    Open the email →
  6. Step 6 · Lead follow-up

    Hot or cold, each lead gets the email that fits

    Follow-ups went out from web form submissions. Someone who asked about SmartStand got an invite to talk to a specialist. Someone who downloaded content got short EasyDirect videos to keep them warm.

    My partSetting up the hot and cold follow-ups from form submissions, and submitting test forms to confirm they fired.

  7. Step 7 · Webinars

    Webinars ran the same way

    Webinars used the same pieces: an invite on the same template, a landing page with a sign-up form, and hot and cold follow-ups after. This invite was for Good Pipetting Practice training, a separate campaign.

    My partSending webinar invites and the follow-ups for people who signed up.
    Open the email →
  8. Step 8 · 23 markets and results

    Translate, then measure

    Pages went out to 23 markets through Welocalize. Conversion and engagement were tracked in Adobe Analytics and Google Analytics, and the next send was built from what worked.

    My partRunning localization for 23 markets and building the reporting dashboards.
    Before it went outchecklist
    • Dates, links and copy match across email and page
    • Test forms submitted, follow-ups fired
    • Translated pages checked against English
    • Tracking in place for the dashboards

Where it carries over

Any B2B launch runs on the same pieces: a product page, an announcement, content behind a form, and a follow-up that fits each lead.
