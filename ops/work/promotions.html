The job

Promotions like free shipping, GapCash, Black Friday and Cyber Monday ran on fixed dates across four brands. Each one had to be built, checked and handed to deployment on time.

What I did

  1. Built and deployed 10,000+ promotional and lifecycle campaigns as part of a team of 15
  2. Added Movable Ink modules like countdown timers, geo-targeting and inventory feeds

  4. Ran Litmus QA for rendering, links, tracking and CAN-SPAM
  5. Managed handoffs between producers and developers in Asana

How it helped

A steady build-and-check routine let the team hit fixed promo dates across four brands, peak season included.
