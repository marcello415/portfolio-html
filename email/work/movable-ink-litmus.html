Movable Ink & Litmus QA

Adding live content with Movable Ink and checking every email in Litmus before it went out.

Company
Gap Inc
Role
Email Developer
Years
2019–23
  • 3Movable Ink module types
  • LitmusQA on every email
  • CAN-SPAMchecked before handoff

The job

Live content made emails more useful, but it gave them more ways to break. Every email needed checking across clients before deployment.

What I did

  1. Added Movable Ink countdown timers, geo-targeting and inventory feeds to SFMC templates
  2. Ran Litmus QA for rendering across email clients and devices
  3. Checked links, tracking and accessibility on every build
  4. Checked CAN-SPAM requirements before handoff
  5. Logged and fixed issues before emails went to deployment

Live content, up close

Movable Ink images are built when the email is opened, not when it’s sent. That makes them useful, and it means they can break in ways a normal image can’t.

Countdown timersReal urgency

Timers counted down to the end of a sale at the moment each customer opened the email.

Geo and inventoryNearest store, live stock

Blocks showed the closest store and products that were still in stock.

How I built it

  1. Placed Movable Ink blocks into SFMC templates with their tracking parameters
  2. Kept the “display images” fallback text for customers with images turned off
  3. Set up Movable Ink content for A/B tests, such as with and without a timer

How I checked it

  1. Ran Litmus across Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail, mobile and dark mode
  2. Checked live blocks showed the right timer, store and products
  3. Clicked every link and confirmed tracking
  4. Checked the unsubscribe link and mailing address for CAN-SPAM
  5. Logged issues and re-tested fixes before deployment

How it helped

Catching rendering and link problems in Litmus meant they got fixed before the send, not after.
