Gap loyalty program emails

A storyboard of my work on Gap’s Integrated Loyalty Program (ILP): the member programs, the loyalty blocks in the daily emails, the tests and the QA. Each step shows what happens, with an example, and what my part was.

Where
Gap Inc, as Email Developer
Program
Integrated Loyalty Program (ILP)
Brands
Gap, Gap Factory, Gap Factory Canada
Tools
Salesforce Marketing Cloud, AMPscript, Litmus

Images are from Gap Factory emails I built in 2023. Names and member data are test data.

  1. Step 1 · The program

    One rewards program, every brand

    Gap Inc. moved its brands onto one loyalty program. Every member had a tier, a points balance and rewards, and some also had the Gap card. The emails had to show each member where they stood.

    One lookup per email

    A shared content block looked up the member’s loyalty data once per email: member or not, tier, points, rewards and card. Every loyalty block read from those results.

Where it carries over

Loyalty email works the same way in any industry. Tiers, points and member offers change, but the job doesn’t: look up each member once, show them only what fits, and test every kind of member before the send.
