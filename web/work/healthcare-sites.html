The job

Arcadian ran a large network of healthcare sites. Content had to stay within healthcare regulations and be accessible, and URL changes couldn't break anything.

What I did

  1. Developed and maintained 120 sites in HTML, CSS, PHP and JavaScript
  2. Partnered with legal and compliance teams to keep content within regulatory guidelines
  3. Ran WCAG, ADA and Section 508 audits and standardized accessible UI components
  4. Built responsive landing pages and web templates
  5. Handled 301 redirects, URL updates and domain management

120 sites, up close

Arcadian’s network included sites for health plans, providers, brokers, careers and news. They shared the same building blocks, so one change could reach every site, for better or worse.
