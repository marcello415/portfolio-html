Content fragments, assets and a launch, working together

A working demo of how I put launch pages together in AEM: copy and links live in content fragments, images come from the asset library, and the next release is staged in a launch until it goes live. Northwind Audio is a made-up brand. The steps are the ones I followed.

Try it: switch locales, turn on Launch preview, click any part of the page to see the fragment behind it, then open QA and fix what it finds before you promote the launch.

Each block of copy on the page comes from a fragment. Fields marked LAUNCH differ in the launch.

Images live once in the asset library. Fragments point to them, and the page loads the rendition that fits the screen.

The checks I ran in UAT before a launch went live, across every locale: required copy filled in, links pointing to their own locale, footnotes matched to their references, and assets in place.
