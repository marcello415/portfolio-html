The job

Each brand had a steady stream of promotions with fixed send dates. Every one needed sign-off from creative, marketing and data teams before it could go out.

What I did

  1. Scheduled and delivered 10,000+ customer communications across multiple brands on deadline

How it helped

Checking the data and reviewing every project before release meant mistakes were caught before customers ever saw them.
