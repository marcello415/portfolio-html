Data Entry & Records

Entering and checking product records for the Apple Online Store and Apple Store app across 88 countries, on fixed deadlines.

The job

Product records had to go live in 88 countries, with the right codes, colors and sizes in every market. Requests came in from marketing, localization and publishing teams, each with its own deadline.

What I did

  1. Processed up to 1,000 product records a month, handling data entry and release on fixed deadlines
  2. Kept product codes, colors and sizes accurate in a database across markets
  3. Tracked and completed requests from several teams in a ticketing system
  4. Coordinated translation workflows and checked content for accuracy before go-live
  5. Followed documented procedures and checklists on every request
  6. Gave status updates and raised blockers in daily team stand-ups

How it helped

Using the same checklist on every request kept records consistent across markets, no matter who on the team picked the request up.
